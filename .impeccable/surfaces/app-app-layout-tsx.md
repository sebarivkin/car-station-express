Scope: the whole operating app (shell, búsqueda, ficha de vehículo, formularios de service/taller, deudas, artículos, empleados, dashboard). Mode: Operate. Code-led (no image generation).

Audience/task: Franco registers jobs at the counter PC and from a phone in the workshop; finds a car by owner/make/model/plate; reads profit by area.

## Direction contract
THESIS: A warm, friendly working app where the one saturated colour (the logo red) appears only where something can be pressed; refuses the grey admin template with red sprinkled everywhere.
OWN-WORLD: Warm cream ground (logo paper) with white cards in light; warm charcoal ground with lifted charcoal cards in dark. Large 14px card radii, generous padding, Nunito throughout with heavy italic 900 for headings echoing the logo wordmark. Red reserved for primary actions, the active nav item and the brand band; state colours (green pagado, amber pendiente) only on payment status. Lucide icons, one stroke.
STORY: Franco opens the app, types a plate or name, lands on the car, taps "Nuevo service" or "Nuevo trabajo", fills it in under a minute with live totals, and checks the dashboard at day's end.
FIRST VIEWPORT: Desktop: left sidebar with logo and nav, main column with a big search field on top, recent operations list below, quick actions to the right. Mobile: top bar with logo + theme toggle, search field first, cards in one column, persistent bottom bar (Buscar, Nuevo, Deudas, Dashboard, Más) reachable by thumb.
FORM: chosen form: challenger digital-design-canon-warm-consumer-app-surface (user choice over assigned candidate 4, gas-station price totem). seed:c556849b10a2765c
Raise (from emission-line-rail, declined): state never by hue alone — pendiente carries an icon and label, not only amber.
